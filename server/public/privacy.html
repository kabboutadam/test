Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

BusMapp helps schools, their drivers, and parents follow the school bus in real time. This policy explains what information BusMapp handles, why, and the choices you have. Plain language, no surprises.

Who does what

Your school decides which children ride which bus and enters their details. For that information the school is responsible for what it collects and how it uses it; BusMapp processes it on the school's behalf to provide the tracking service. BusMapp provides the app and safeguards the data.

What we collect

Only what the service needs:

  • Phone numbers — so you (parent, driver, or school staff) can sign in with a texted code, and to identify who you are.
  • Child details, entered by the school — name, grade/class, the home pickup address and its map location, and which bus the child rides.
  • Parent details — name and phone number, linked to your child.
  • Driver & vehicle details — driver name, phone, the bus's plate, and the bus's live GPS location while a route is running.
  • Notification tokens — so we can send "bus is near" alerts to your device.

We do not collect payments inside the app, and we do not ask children to use the app themselves.

How we use it

  • Show parents their own child's bus on a live map, with pickup times and "stops away."
  • Let drivers see their route's pickup list and share the bus's location while driving.
  • Send login codes and arrival notifications.
  • Keep each school, family, and driver seeing only what's theirs.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Location, specifically

  • A bus's location is shared only while the driver has an active route, and only with the parents of children on that bus and the school. When the route ends, live sharing stops.
  • A child's home location (the pickup pin) is used to place the stop and calculate pickup times. It is visible to the school and to the driver of that child's bus, so the pickup can happen.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted providers to run the service:

  • SMS delivery — to text login codes.
  • Maps & routing — maps display on your device; pickup coordinates are used to estimate drive times.
  • Hosting & database — to run the service and store data securely.

These providers process data only to provide their part of the service. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of a child or others.

How long we keep it

We keep information while the school is an active customer and the child rides the bus. When a child leaves, a school stops using BusMapp, or you ask us to delete your data, we remove it within a reasonable period — except where we must keep it to meet a legal obligation.

Security

Sign-in is by one-time SMS code — there are no passwords to leak. Access is scoped by role: parents see only their own child, drivers only their own bus, schools only their own students. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Parents — ask your school to update or remove your or your child's details, or contact us.
  • Location — drivers can stop sharing at any time by ending the route; the app only tracks during an active route.
  • Notifications — turn them off in your phone settings.

Children

BusMapp is a tool for schools and parents, not a service children sign up for or use on their own. Information about children is provided by the school under its agreement with families. If you believe a child's information is in BusMapp without proper authorization, contact us and we will address it.
